Start with a Sprint.

A Sprint is one week at a fixed scope and a fixed price. One of your workflows runs end to end by Friday, with your people reviewing what comes out. Write to hello@atlr.pro with the workflow you have in mind and a founder replies within one business day.

Tell us the workflow

A founder replies within one business day, Toronto hours. What you type goes to hello@atlr.pro and nowhere else, as the Privacy page says.

Name, work email and the workflow are enough.

What a Sprint needs from you
  • A workflow you would like to see run, ideally the one that eats the most skilled hours each week.
  • The people who decide on it today, half a day of their time on Monday and an hour on Friday.
  • The shape of the data it touches. Synthetic data is fine for the week.
  • The systems it has to talk to.
  • The number you would measure it by.

If you run a production rather than a department, the workflow is an episode and the network’s spec.

We sign your NDA, or ours, before the brief. Keynotes and Materiality are booked through AI Imagine Labs.
