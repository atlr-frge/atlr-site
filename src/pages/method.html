Four moves, in order, every time. The Sprint comes first, so you see something working on your own workflow before you commit to anything else.

00
The Forge Sprint

One week, one system, built on your workflow and connected to your infrastructure, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. By Friday it runs end to end in a demo environment and you watch it work, at a fixed scope and a fixed price agreed before Monday.

If it is right, we go further. If it is not, you have lost a week rather than a year.

01
Learn

Time inside the domain first: the workflow as it actually moves, the data and where it lives, and the people who decide. We talk to the team, map the systems, and find the constraints nobody else asks about, and an outsider’s reading of your field is part of the method.

Two to four weeks of embedded discovery before we scope anything larger than the Sprint.

02
Build

On the real workflow, inside your cloud, on the models you approve, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. A synthetic population lets a prototype exist before data access is approved, and the scope stays honest about what works and what does not yet.

You see it running on your own data before we move past this stage.

03
Prove

Working end to end in a demo environment, behind automated tests, before production. The system is measured against the number you care about and against your own records, and the miss rate is reported to the person who owns the outcome.

Every engagement ships an evidence pack for whoever owns model risk.

04
Stay

Maintained after hand-off and improved from what your reviewers accept and reject. The system takes on more of your workflow and more of your stack every month, and what you have in month twelve is a different order of magnitude from month one.

Most clients expand scope within the first two quarters.

The Sprint, in detail

What a week produces.

What you see on Friday

One workflow running end to end in a demo environment on our cloud: your documents and data shapes loaded as context, the agents drafting their outputs with evidence attached, a review queue where your people accept or reject what comes through, and a record of every call. We measure it against the baseline you gave us on Monday and show you both numbers.

Monday brief and baseline → Tuesday to Thursday build → Friday you watch it run

What we need from you

A workflow you would like to see run, the people who decide on it, the shape of the data it touches (synthetic is fine for the week), the systems it has to talk to, and the number you would measure it by. Half a day of your team’s time on Monday and an hour on Friday is the usual cost to you.

Fixed scope · fixed price · agreed before the week starts
Beyond simple prompts

Systems that understand the goal behind the ask.

Most deployed systems follow instructions literally, which is useful and limited. Ours hold the business outcome behind the instruction, know what good looks like, catch drift, and adjust before anyone has to step in.

When confidence is low they stop and ask rather than guess, and every call and decision is written to a record a reviewer or a regulator can read. Model output reaches a decision by one route only: the draft a person edits and approves.
