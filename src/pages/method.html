The Forge Sprint

A Sprint is one week at a fixed scope and a fixed price, agreed before Monday. One of your workflows runs end to end by Friday, in a demo environment on our cloud, on synthetic data unless you choose otherwise, with your people reviewing what comes out. The build that follows runs inside your cloud.

If it is right, we go further. If it is not, you have lost a week rather than a year.

01
Learn

Time inside the domain first: the workflow as it moves, the data and where it lives, and the people who decide. We sit with the team and find the constraints nobody else asks about.

Two to four weeks of embedded discovery open the build and come before anything larger than the Sprint is scoped.

02
Build

On the real workflow, inside your cloud, on the models you approve, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet, and a scope that names what runs and what is still to be built.

You see it running on your own data before we move past this stage.

03
Prove

Working end to end in a demo environment, behind automated tests, before production. The system is measured against the number you care about, and before go-live it runs against your own records with the miss rate reported to the person who owns the outcome.

Every engagement ships an evidence pack for whoever owns model risk.

04
Stay

Maintained after hand-off and improved from what your reviewers accept and reject. The system takes on more of your workflow and more of your stack each month; one compliance platform has taken four systems in sequence since 2025.

Each addition is priced as its own system.

What a week produces.

What you see on Friday

One workflow running end to end in a demo environment on our cloud, connected to the systems you name, with synthetic data wherever real data cannot be used yet. The agents draft with evidence attached, your people accept or reject what comes through, every call is on record, and we measure it against the baseline you gave us on Monday.

Monday brief and baseline → Tuesday to Thursday build → Friday you watch it run

What we need from you

A workflow you would like to see run, the people who decide on it, the shape of the data it touches (synthetic is fine for the week), the systems it has to talk to, and the number you would measure it by. Half a day of your team’s time on Monday and an hour on Friday is the cost to you.

Fixed scope · fixed price · agreed before the week starts
Two Sprints

As a week would run.

One for a compliance team, one for a production.

For a compliance team

The workflow is this month’s alerts into cases. On Monday we load your typologies, policy and thresholds over a synthetic population shaped like your members. By Friday the agents have drafted each case with its evidence and a confidence score, the low-confidence cases are waiting for the analyst, and nothing has been filed without the compliance officer. You measure it against the hours a case takes today.

Monday typologies, policy and thresholds in → Friday cases drafted, the low-confidence ones held for the analyst

For a production

The workflow is one episode’s AI shots against the network’s delivery specification. On Monday we take the shots, the spec and the edit. By Friday every shot has been through the QC gate, is traced to its prompt, tool and date, and the disclosure record is drafted with a person’s sign-off on every row. You measure it against the notes the network would otherwise send back.

Monday shots, spec and edit in → Friday QC report and disclosure record, signed per row
