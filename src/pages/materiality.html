The word

Material means important enough to change the decision.

In securities law and accounting, materiality is the threshold at which a piece of information becomes important enough to change a decision-maker’s judgment. The machine now surfaces everything, and almost none of it is material. Deciding which part is remains a human call, and that call is the series.

How it is made

Built on the same engine.

Materiality is produced by AI Imagine Labs and Atelier Superintelligence with Verma Private Wealth at Canaccord Genuity. Atelier’s Conformer engine holds the series’ visual canon, candidate portraits wait for a person to crown or reject them, and the same records and review sit behind every frame as behind every client build.
