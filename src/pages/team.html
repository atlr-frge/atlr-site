The team

Two founders, and the engineers they have worked with for years.

Atelier is the workshop Sean Evans and Michael Chase run for building agentic systems. Sean leads engineering and production, Michael leads strategy and adoption, and artists and engineers build together.

Michael Chase

Co-founder · Strategy and adoption

Michael was Senior Advisor and Futurist at Deloitte from 2022 to 2025, where he taught AI to partners at Deloitte University and to clients at the Greenhouse, developed the Nvidia digital-twin work for the BMW and Mercedes EV plants, and worked with Desjardins on AI in its call centres.

Before Deloitte he founded TRAVAI, an AI travel startup, sold it to Kognitiv, and stayed on as Partner and Chief Strategy Officer, where he built the Kognitiv loyalty engine and developed PC Travel. Earlier, as a Forbes Top 100 CMO, he built Cup of Jo for St. Joseph into a franchise with millions of views and counted Google, Thomson Reuters and Nike as clients.

He hosts Materiality, the weekly series on AI with Verma Private Wealth at Canaccord Genuity, writes the Monday Morning Mashup at AI Imagine Labs, and speaks to boards and conferences: the keynote at TiECon Canada 2026, with CES, SXSW, NRF Big Show, the Digital Signage Expo and a two-year cross-Canada tour for Canada Post among earlier stages.

Sean Evans

Co-founder · Engineering and production

Sean has spent 28 years building production systems: pipeline architect on broadcast series with thousands of VFX shots, co-founder of Occupied VR with capture and volumetric pipelines for Samsung and others, and machine learning in his pipelines since 2016.

He executive produced The Tragically Hip: No Dress Rehearsal, released on Prime Video, and as Animation Director built the AI pipeline that turned thousands of archival photographs into 4K motion. The series had its world premiere at TIFF 2024, took the People’s Choice Documentary Award there, and won seven Canadian Screen Awards, every category it was nominated in. He executive produced The Holy City, a VR pilgrimage built from laser scans and photogrammetry of Jerusalem’s holy sites, an official selection at Cannes XR 2019 and winner of the Advanced Imaging Society’s Lumière award for Best Educational XR, and produced Spots of Light, a volumetric documentary of real people that you walk through in a headset, in competition at Venice Immersive 2023 and shown in the SIGGRAPH 2024 VR Theater.

In 2026 he was brought on mid-production to Billy Bob Thornton Presents: The Mayflower on Fox Nation, for its AI pipeline, delivery and quality control. For Daniel Lanois and Warner Music he built the album films, the tour campaign and the archive agent. Since 2025 he has built investigation agents, detection, an operations console and a KYC module with a Canadian compliance platform’s team, and in 2017 he did the digital production of Dean Drever’s Eagle at 1 The Esplanade, Toronto. He is a Webby Awards juror, a member of the International Academy of Digital Arts and Sciences, and has spoken at SXSW and SIGGRAPH.
