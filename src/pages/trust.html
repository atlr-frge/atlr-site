Trust

The questions a model-risk team asks first.

Short answers, each backed by one of the five controls that run on every build. Where an answer is set per engagement, it says so.

01Where does it run?

Inside your cloud tenant, on the models you approve. A Sprint runs in a demo environment on Atelier’s cloud, on synthetic data unless you choose otherwise, so a prototype can exist before data access is approved.

02Which models, and where?

The ones on your allow-list. Routing is restricted in code to approved regions, Canadian regions where the work requires it, and the cost of each run is recorded where the provider reports it.

03What happens to personal information?

It is tokenised before any model call. In the financial-crime work no member data was needed to build or benchmark the detectors; a synthetic population with criminal typologies seeded in stood in for it.

04What is logged?

Every model call and every human decision, written to a hash-chained audit record. Each generated output keeps its model, prompt and settings, and each accept or reject is logged with who and when.

05Who approves what goes out?

A person, by one route only: the draft they edit and approve. When the system’s confidence falls below the threshold you set, it stops and escalates rather than guessing, and the reason is logged for the reviewer.

06How are releases controlled?

Every release passes automated tests and a health check before it rolls out. Every delivery is a new numbered package, cut by a script that refuses to overwrite, with every copy checked by SHA-256.

07How is our work kept apart from other clients’?

One instance per client or production, with its own database, storage and login. Inputs are hash-checked against their source, and review sites decrypt only in the viewer’s browser.

08What do we get for model risk?

An evidence pack assembled from these records ships with every engagement, for whoever owns model risk. Before go-live at an institution, the system runs against that institution’s own filed records and the miss rate goes to the person who owns the outcome.

09Who owns what is built?

You own your data and the tuning built on it. Ownership of the code written for you, and the licence under which Atelier’s own engine, Conformer and Conduit, is brought into a job, are set in the statement of work before the first hour.

10Who does the work, and under what terms?

The two founders and the engineers and artists they have shipped with before, scaled to the scope, all under the confidentiality terms of the engagement. We sign your NDA, or ours, before the brief.
